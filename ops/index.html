Load Breaker

Split it any way you want

A pallet is rarely worth the most sold whole. Break it into smaller pallets, boxes or single pieces, in any mix. LotMind divides what you paid, plus the shipping in, across every lot by retail value, so each one knows its true cost.

  • Pick the load and say how you want it split.
  • Each lot gets its share of the cost.
  • Every lot stays tied to its purchase order.

Lister

List from a photo

LotMind writes the listing from your photos: the title, the description, the count and the price. It goes live on your LotMind store and your Shopify store, so you never copy a listing from one place to another.

  • Take the pictures. LotMind writes it; you approve it.
  • It goes live on every store you sell from.
  • No store yet? You get one, ready to sell.

LotMind Bins

Find anything, anywhere

You do not need racking or a label on every shelf. Take one photo of the floor, lay a grid on it, and tap where the pallet sits. Pick lists show each lot's spot and photo, and anything never put away shows up in red.

  • Set up an area once: a rack, a shelf or a floor photo.
  • At receiving, scan the load and tap its spot.
  • Pick lists with a spot and photo for every lot.

Product analytics

What's hot, what's not, from real sales

See what buyers are looking at, carting and buying this week, and what's sitting. Every lot shows its views, carts, open quotes and days on the shelf, and every brand and category shows its sell-through and price a piece, all from your own orders, not estimates. Lotti suggests what to do with each slow lot.

  • Hot lots and lots that aren't moving.
  • Views, carts, quotes and days to sell per lot.
  • Sell-through and price a piece by brand and category.

Labels

Scan to see inside

One label carries a QR code and the product barcode. The QR opens the live listing, so a buyer on your floor sees photos, what is inside and today's price without opening the box.

  • Bin labels, tags, box stickers or pallet labels.
  • A buyer scans it with their phone camera.
  • The price is never out of date.

Ship

Know what has to leave today

Every paid order waiting to ship, every freight quote a buyer is waiting on, and every load on the road, on one screen, with a score on how fast you ship.

  • A buyer pays. The order appears under To ship.
  • Freight quotes waiting get answered first.
  • Days to ship, and freight collected against freight paid.

Books

Every broken load rolls back up

A load gets broken into dozens of lots, and each one sells on its own. Books rolls every lot back up to the load it came from: what you paid, the shipping in, what each lot sold for, and what is still on the floor. You see what every load really made, and the day-to-day books (money in, what buyers owe, what you owe suppliers, profit for the month) fill themselves in from the same orders.

  • Each lot's sale rolls back up to its load.
  • See profit per load: paid, sold, still on hand.
  • Orders, payments and supplier invoices post on their own.
