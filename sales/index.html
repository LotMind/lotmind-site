The agent

Every message answered, day and night

Buyers write when it suits them. The agent answers in seconds on the same channel, asks what they sell and how much they move, and remembers it. Anything it shouldn't handle alone goes to your team.

  • Answers in about two minutes, any hour.
  • Interviews new buyers and tags what they buy.
  • Hands refunds, complaints and requests for a person to your team.

Offers and quotes

Drag-and-drop offer pages. Quotes that write themselves.

Drag lots into an offer and LotMind builds a branded offer page with photos, manifests and price a piece. When a buyer asks, the quote writes itself from the lot, the price per piece, box shipping or a real freight rate, and is checked before it goes out. When new stock lands, it picks the buyers most likely to want it and writes each offer.

  • Drag lots into a branded offer page.
  • Automatic quotes, never below your floor.
  • Accepted deals wait for your OK, then the checkout link goes out.

Buyer analytics

Know every buyer from their real orders

Every buyer gets a profile built from what they actually bought and said: how often they buy and when they're due, average order, what goods and lot sizes they want, where they sell, and their budget. LotMind tags each buyer automatically (buyer type, goods, lot size, channel, region), so you can find the right buyers for a lot in seconds.

  • Purchase frequency and who's due to buy again.
  • Automatic buyer tags from orders and conversations.
  • Average order, what they buy, where they sell.

Connections

Everything right in front of you

Sales questions and customer service from email, text messages, Facebook, Instagram, WhatsApp, store chat and the phone all land in one inbox, on the buyer's profile, with their orders beside them. The inbox is tied to your real email, so every reply goes out from your own address and lands in your own sent mail. Connect each channel with one button, including Shopify, eBay and Amazon. Nobody types a password into the agent.

  • Tied to your real email: replies go out from your address.
  • Email, texts, DMs, chat and calls in one inbox.
  • Customer service and sales side by side, on the buyer.
  • Connect or disconnect each channel with one button.

AI receptionist, voice, texting, click to call

An AI receptionist that answers every call

Your agent answers your main line with an AI voice, like a receptionist who never takes a break. Buyers get help on the spot: questions, quotes, booked calls. Everyone else is routed the way you set it: shippers to your shipping person, accounting to your bookkeeper, anyone who wants a person to your team, and messages taken for the rest. It makes follow-up calls too. Texting (SMS) is built in. From any buyer, one click calls them or emails them. Every call is recorded, transcribed and summarized on the buyer's profile.

  • AI receptionist: answers, routes and takes messages.
  • AI voice on your phone line, in and out.
  • SMS texting built in.
  • Click to call and click to email from any buyer.
  • Every call transcribed and summarized.

Service

Problems found before the buyer asks twice

The agent reads every email, text and DM for short counts, damage, late deliveries and refund trouble, and puts each on a list with who has it. The owner approves any store credit.

  • Problems are pulled out of messages automatically.
  • A teammate answers or proposes a credit.
  • You see what's open and what was given.
