Flex Bins Privacy Policy

Last updated: [date it goes live]

LotMind, Inc. [confirm the legal name], a California corporation (“LotMind”, “we”), makes the Flex Bins app for Shopify stores. This policy explains what information Flex Bins collects when a store installs it, how we use it, and the choices stores and their customers have.

1. Information we collect

  • From your Shopify store: your store’s name and address, products, product variants, inventory levels, locations and orders, through the Shopify permissions you approve when you install the app.
  • What you add in the app: bins, areas, racks, load and purchase order details, quantities, prices and notes.
  • Photos you upload: pictures of paperwork (purchase orders, bills of lading) and of storage areas.
  • Your team: the email address of the staff member who makes a change, so the app can show who moved or received stock.
  • Customer information: Flex Bins does not store your customers’ names, emails, phone numbers or addresses. Pick lists are read live from Shopify. The only customer-related record we keep is an order’s ID on a stock movement.

2. How we use it

Only to run Flex Bins for your store: to track where stock is, receive loads, build pick lists, show your Scoreboard, read the paperwork photos you upload, and bill you through Shopify. We do not sell your information or use it for advertising.

3. Service providers

  • Google Cloud hosts the app, its database and your photos (stored privately and shown through short-lived links).
  • Google Vertex AI reads the paperwork photos you ask Flex Bins to read. Photos are sent only when you request a read.
  • Shopify handles installs and billing.

Each provider processes data only to provide its service to us.

4. How long we keep it

For as long as your store has Flex Bins installed. When you uninstall, Shopify notifies us 48 hours later and we delete all of your store’s data, including photos. When Shopify sends us a customer data deletion request, we remove that customer’s order IDs from our records.

5. Your choices and rights

Stores can see and change their data in the app, and can ask us to export or delete it. Your customers’ requests come to us through Shopify, and we answer them as Shopify requires. Depending on where you live, laws such as the California Consumer Privacy Act may give you more rights. [Lawyer: confirm the list for CCPA and GDPR.]

6. Security

Data is encrypted in transit, photos are stored privately, and each store can see only its own data.

7. Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date above and tell stores in the app for material changes.
