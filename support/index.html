Getting started with Flex Bins

  • Pick a plan: every plan has a 14-day free trial, billed through Shopify.
  • Set up your first rack: the Welcome screen asks for your layout, makes your first bins and brings in your listed products.
  • Areas without racking: take a photo of the area and drop pins on it, or lay a grid over it.
  • Receiving: photograph the purchase order or bill of lading and Flex Bins reads it. Check what it read before you save.

Billing

Charges, plan changes and cancellations are handled in your Shopify admin under Settings, Billing. Uninstalling the app ends your plan.
