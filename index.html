Paperless, with little to no data entry

No clipboards. No paper POs. Almost nothing to type.

Every step of a load happens on a phone or a screen. The camera, the email and the manifest do the data entry, and every document files itself on the load. The only paper left is the label on the box, and buyers scan that to see the live listing.

BuyingSeller emails and manifests are read; POs, invoices and bills of lading file themselves.
ReceivingScan the load on a phone. No count sheets, no clipboard.
BreakingSplit and cost lots on screen. No spreadsheets to re-type.
LocatingSpots set by photo and tap. No location binders.
Picking and shippingPick lists with a photo of each lot, on the phone.
SellingOffers, counters, orders and store credits sent digitally.
BooksEvery sale rolls back up to its load. No ledgers to fill in.
