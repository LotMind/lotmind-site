You buy pallets of returned and overstock goods. How it works

Value it. Buy it. Break it. List it. Locate it. Sell it.

Every item stays linked to the purchase order it came from, so you always know what you paid and what it sold for. And what sells, for how much, helps price the next load you buy.

Deal Desk

Buy from your inbox

Sellers already email you their loads. LotMind works right inside your email.

Inbound dealsValue · offer · file

  • Reads your deal emails

    When a seller emails you a load, LotMind reads the email and the item list.

  • You pick which ones to price

    It tells you what each load is worth and the most you should pay for it.

  • Offer in one click

    Send your offer back to the seller with one click.

  • Paperwork attached

    The invoice, item list and shipping papers are saved with the purchase order for you.

  • Shipping logged

    Every shipping email, from pickup to delivery, is saved with the load.

  • All from your mail

    Nothing new to learn. You stay in your email and the purchase order keeps itself up to date.

Load Breaker

Split it any way you want

Bought a whole pallet? Sell it however makes the most money.

Breaking a palletPiece · box · pallet

  • By the piece, box or pallet

    Break it down into single items, boxes or smaller pallets, or any mix.

  • A fair cost for every lot

    What you paid, plus shipping, is divided across every lot, so each one knows its true cost.

  • Still tied to the purchase order

    Every lot remembers which purchase order it came from, so you can see what each load really made.

Lister

List from a photo

Take a picture. LotMind writes the listing.

Photo to listingNo typing

  • Written for you

    The title, description and price are filled in from the photo.

  • Sell everywhere

    It goes live on your Shopify store, and from there to other marketplaces.

  • We'll build your store

    No Shopify store yet? We set one up for you, ready to sell.

Flex Bins

Find anything, anywhere

Racks, floor spots or no racking at all.

Where everything isChange it anytime

  • Any spot can be a bin

    A shelf, a rack, a corner of the floor. Set up one rack or the whole warehouse.

  • Photo bins

    No racking? Snap a photo of where you set it down, and that photo is the location.

  • Move things around

    Add a row, take one away, rearrange. Nothing to relabel.

  • Pick lists with photos

    When an order comes in, you get a list of where each item is, with the photo.

  • Scan to put away

    Scan the load, scan the bin, done.

  • Nothing goes missing

    Anything that came in but isn't in a bin or sold shows up in red.

Labeling

Scan to see inside

No more opening boxes to show a buyer what's in them.

Smart labelsQR + barcode

  • One label

    The QR code and the product barcode print together.

  • Buyers scan it

    Buyers in your warehouse scan the QR with their phone and see photos, what's inside and the price.

  • Always today's price

    The QR opens the live listing, so the price is always current.

Customer Intelligence Agent

Not just a chatbot

An AI salesperson that knows your buyers and your stock, and closes deals while you work the floor.

Customer Intelligence AgentYour AI salesperson

  • Knows your buyers

    It remembers every buyer: what they looked at, asked about, bought and paid.

  • Knows everything you have

    Every item, where it is and what it cost, so it only offers what's really for sale, at your prices.

  • Negotiates and closes

    It answers by text, email, chat and social media, haggles within your limits, and closes the sale.

  • Watches what's hot

    It sees what's selling and what isn't, and offers the right lots to the right buyers.

  • Feeds the Deal Desk

    Everything it learns helps price your next purchase.

  • Yours to name

    She works under your store's name. Call her whatever you like.
