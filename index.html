LotMind Buy

Buy from your inbox

Sellers already email you their loads. LotMind works right inside your email.

Inbound dealsValue · offer · file

  1. Price a load from your inbox

    Working today

    Sellers already email you their loads, with a list attached. LotMind reads the email and the list, works out what the load should sell for, and tells you the most you should pay. You never retype a manifest to decide on a deal.

    1. A seller emails you a load. It shows up in LotMind with the item list read in.
    2. You pick the ones worth pricing.
    3. LotMind shows what the load is worth and the most to pay for it.
    4. Send your offer back with one click.

  2. Purchase orders that keep themselves up to date

    Working today

    When you buy, the purchase order becomes the home for everything about that load. The invoice, the bill of lading, the manifest and every shipping email are read and filed with it, so the paperwork is never in someone's inbox.

    1. The deal becomes a purchase order.
    2. Paperwork is read into fields for a person to check, and saved with the order.
    3. Shipping emails, from pickup to delivery, are logged on the load.
    4. When the truck arrives, the load is waiting in Flex Bins to be counted and put away.

LotMind Break

Split it any way you want

Bought a whole pallet? Sell it however makes the most money.

Breaking a palletPiece · box · pallet

  1. Split a load and cost every lot

    Working today

    A pallet is rarely worth the most sold whole. Break it into smaller pallets, boxes or single pieces, in any mix. LotMind divides what you paid, plus the shipping in, across every lot, so each one knows its true cost.

    1. Pick the load and say how you want it split.
    2. LotMind makes the lots and gives each its share of the cost.
    3. Every lot stays tied to its purchase order.
    4. When lots sell, you see what the whole load really made.

LotMind SalesYour CRM and your salesperson

  1. Every message answered, day and night

    Working today

    Buyers write when it suits them, on whatever app they use. LotMind Sales answers texts, WhatsApp, Messenger, Instagram, email, your store chat and lead forms from one inbox. It works under your store's name, and you can call it whatever you like.

    1. A buyer writes in, at any hour.
    2. LotMind Sales answers in seconds on the same channel.
    3. It asks what they sell and how much they move, and remembers the answers.
    4. Anything it should not handle alone is handed to your team.

  2. Offers, negotiation and orders

    Working today

    It knows every lot you have, where it is and what it cost, so it only offers what is really for sale. When new stock lands it picks the buyers most likely to want it, haggles inside the limits you set, and writes the order.

    1. New stock lands. LotMind Sales matches it to buyers by what they bought before.
    2. It writes each offer. You click send, or let it send on its own.
    3. It negotiates, never below your floor.
    4. It writes the draft order and sends the checkout link. When the buyer pays, it is an order.

  3. A desk for your sales team

    Working today

    Some buyers only pick up the phone. Each salesperson gets a desk with today's calls, call backs and messages waiting, with what each buyer bought before, and their numbers against the goal.

    1. Buyers that cannot be texted or emailed go on a daily call list.
    2. The salesperson calls, and taps how it went.
    3. Call backs and unanswered messages stay on the desk until someone handles them.
    4. Owners see each person's calls, replies and orders.

LotMind Store

A wholesale store that sells lots, not items

Your own store, built for pallets, boxes and truckloads. Every lot LotMind lists shows up on it with its cover, its count and its price a piece. Buyers shop from their phone and pay through your checkout.

LotMind StoreHow each part works

  1. Photo listing

    Working today

    Listing is the slowest job in a warehouse, so LotMind does it from pictures. You photograph the pallet or the box. LotMind reads what is in the photos, checks it against the manifest when there is one, and writes the listing for you to approve.

    1. Take a picture of the top of the pallet, or of the pieces laid out from a box.
    2. LotMind names the brands and the kinds of goods it sees, and counts them.
    3. It builds the cover from the lot's own photos and writes the title, the description, the count and the price a piece.
    4. You check it. One tap puts it on your store.

  2. Dynamic manifests

    Working today

    A manifest is usually a spreadsheet the buyer has to download and read. On a LotMind store it is part of the lot page: every line with its brand, size, condition and count. Because it is data and not a file, buyers can search your whole store by what is inside the lots, not only by the titles.

    1. Give LotMind the supplier's manifest once.
    2. It ties the lines to the lots they belong to, so each lot carries its own manifest.
    3. The lot page shows the total pieces and the biggest lines first.
    4. A buyer who searches for a brand finds every lot that has it inside.

  3. Auctions

    Working today

    Some lots get looked at again and again and never bought. That usually means the price is the problem. An auction lets your buyers name the price, with a floor you set, and it turns a lot that was sitting into one people compete for.

    1. LotMind suggests lots for auction and says why, such as 36 visits in 30 days and no carts.
    2. You set the starting bid, the reserve and how long it runs.
    3. Each buyer enters the most they would pay. The store bids for them in steps, only as high as it needs to.
    4. A bid in the last five minutes adds five minutes, so nobody wins by sniping.
    5. The highest bid at or over your reserve wins. The winner is emailed and your salesperson writes the order.

  4. Buyer metrics

    Coming next

    A reseller looking at a lot has one question: can I make money on this? Each lot page will show the numbers that answer it, and you will see who is interested before they ever write to you.

    1. The buyer sees how many people are viewing and watching the lot, and what similar lots sold for a piece.
    2. A calculator turns their own selling price and fees into a profit and a break-even price.
    3. They tap Watch to save the lot and hear about a price drop.
    4. You see who is watching each lot and what they did on the page, with one button to reach all of them.

    Coming next

    Buyers trust what they can see. In a live show, someone on your team opens pallets on camera and sells them while buyers watch, ask questions and buy from the same page.

    1. Pick the lots for the show. LotMind suggests the ones that sell best on camera.
    2. Go live from a phone. The lot on camera shows beside the video with its price and a buy button.
    3. Buyers ask questions in the chat and buy or bid without leaving the show.

LotMind Ship

Know what has to leave today

Paid orders, freight quotes and loads on the road, on one screen.

LotMind ShipOrders out the door

  1. One screen for everything going out

    Working today

    A paid order that sits is a buyer who does not come back. LotMind Ship lists every order waiting to ship, every freight quote a buyer is waiting on, and every load on the road, and it keeps score on how fast you ship.

    1. A buyer pays. The order appears under To ship.

LotMind Service

Problems found before the buyer asks twice

Short counts, damage, late deliveries and refunds, tracked from the first message to the fix.

LotMind ServiceIssues and credits

  1. Every order problem, in one list

    Working today

    Order problems hide inside message threads. LotMind reads buyer messages for missing pieces, damage, wrong items, late deliveries and refund trouble, and puts each one on a list with who has it and whether it has been answered.

    1. A buyer writes about a problem. It appears on the Service list with what they said.
    2. A teammate answers, or proposes a store credit.
    3. The owner approves, changes or declines the credit. Approving sends the buyer a gift card.
    4. The issue is marked resolved, and you see how many are open and how much was given.

LotMind Books

Simple books that fill themselves in

The day-to-day money picture, built from what LotMind already knows.

LotMind BooksWhat came in, what you owe

  1. Money in, money owed, profit

    Working today

    LotMind already knows every order, every payment and every purchase order, so the basic books write themselves. You see sales, what buyers still owe, what you owe each supplier and how old it is, and profit for the month.

    1. Orders, payments and refunds post on their own.
    2. Supplier invoices come in from your purchase orders.
    3. You mark an invoice scheduled or paid.
    4. You read what you owe by age and a simple profit and loss. It is a day-to-day view, not a replacement for your accountant.
