How it works

A complete loop for better valuations

From the bid to the last sale, every unit stays tied to the purchase order it came from. Everything the Customer Intelligence Agent sees and sells flows back to the Deal Desk, so every valuation is sharper than the last.

The LotMind loop6 modules · 1 purchase order
  1. Deal DeskPhotograph the manifest, or let it read the deal emails in your inbox. Pick a deal and it tells you what the load is worth and what to pay, then makes the offer in a click and opens the purchase order, with the paperwork attached. More below.value & buy it
  2. Load BreakerBought it as a pallet? Break it down to any increment you want: by the piece, the box or the pallet. Each lot gets its share of the cost, freight included.break it
  3. ShopifyLots list themselves, photographed and priced. From Shopify they sell on any marketplace. No store yet? We build your Shopify store for you, ready to sell.list it
  4. Flex BinsFlex bins: photo-locate your whole warehouse or a single rack, on the fly. A bin can be a racked shelf, a spot on the floor, or just a photo of where the lot sits. Add a row, take it away: completely flexible, no relabeling. Pick lists come straight from orders and show the photo.locate it
  5. LabelingPrint labels with the load's QR code and the product barcode in one step. Buyers on your floor scan the QR and the live listing comes up: photos, what's inside, price. No more opening boxes to see what's inside.label it
  6. Customer Intelligence AgentNot just a chatbot. Your own AI sales agent knows your buyers and your entire inventory, negotiates and closes on her own. More below.sell it
  7. Full circleThe Customer Intelligence Agent feeds it all back to the Deal Desk: what's hot, what's not, what sold, for how much and how fast. Every sale sharpens the next bid.learn from it

Deal Desk

Buy from your inbox

The loop starts where your deals already arrive: your email.

Inbound dealsValue · offer · file

  • Reads your deal emails

    Load offers from your suppliers land in your inbox. The Deal Desk reads them, manifests and all.

  • You choose what to value

    Pick the deals worth a look and it tells you what each load is worth and what to pay.

  • Offer in one click

    Create the offer and send it back from your mail with a single click.

  • Paperwork attached

    Invoice, manifest and shipping documents attach to the purchase order automatically.

  • Shipping logged

    Every shipping email, from pickup to delivery, is logged to the load.

  • All from your mail

    No new portal to learn. You work from your inbox and the purchase order keeps itself up to date.

Customer Intelligence Agent

Not just a chatbot

An autonomous AI sales agent that knows your buyers, knows your inventory, and closes deals while you run the floor.

Customer Intelligence AgentWorks 24/7

  • Knows your buyers

    What each buyer is looking at right now, and their whole history with you: what they bought, what they asked about, what they paid.

  • Knows your entire inventory

    Every lot, every bin, every cost and what's live, so she only offers what you actually have, at prices you set.

  • Negotiates and closes

    Answers by text, DM, email and chat, counters within your rules, and closes the sale on her own.

  • Watches what's hot

    Tracks every listing to see what's moving and what's not, and pushes the right lots to the right buyers.

  • Feeds the Deal Desk

    Everything she sees and sells goes back to the Deal Desk, so every valuation is sharper than the last.

  • Yours to name

    She works under your store's name. Call her whatever you like.
