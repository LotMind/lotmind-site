LotMind Rep

The CRM with a brain

It doesn't just track your buyers. It sells to them. Your own AI sales rep and assistant answers every message, offers the right loads, negotiates, and does the back office: quotes, offers and Shopify draft orders, day and night.

LotMind RepYour CRM and your salesperson

  • Every buyer, remembered

    What they bought, how often they reorder, what they passed on, and how you're allowed to reach them. Your CRM, built in.

  • One inbox, every channel

    Texts, WhatsApp, Messenger, Instagram, email, your store chat and lead forms, all answered in one place, day and night.

  • Asks the right questions

    It finds out what each buyer runs, what they sell and how much they move, then ranks and groups them for you.

  • Offers the right load

    When new stock lands, it picks the buyers most likely to want it and writes each offer. Click to send, or let it send on its own.

  • Negotiates and closes

    It haggles within your limits, writes the Shopify draft order and sends the checkout link. When the buyer pays, it's an order.

  • Hands your team the calls

    Buyers it can't text or email go on a daily call list for your team, with what each one bought before.

  • Knows everything you have

    Every item, where it is and what it cost, so it only offers what's really for sale, at your prices.

  • Feeds the Deal Desk

    What sells, and for how much, helps price your next purchase.

  • Yours to name

    She works under your store's name. Call her whatever you like.
